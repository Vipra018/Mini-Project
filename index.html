About Haze-Analyzer

Haze-Analyzer is an AI-powered vision-based Air Quality Index estimation platform designed to predict air pollution levels using street-level images captured from mobile devices.

Vision-Based Detection

Uses computer vision and deep learning to analyze haze, visibility, and environmental patterns from images.

AI Powered

Integrates CNN-based machine learning models trained on AQI datasets such as PM25Vision.

Real-Time Monitoring

Enables low-cost and scalable pollution monitoring in areas with limited sensor infrastructure.
